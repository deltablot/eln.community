Welcome to ELN.community

This template sharing site is maintained by Deltablot (home of eLabFTW) to allow users of electronic laboratory notebooks to easily share experiment and resource templates.
All templates are uploaded in .eln file format (CC by 4.0 license) and assigned a persistent identifier with versioning to facilitate citing.

Please note that we do not store your email address. To receive notifications, make sure your email address is set to public on orcid.org

For issues and features requests, please visit our Github page.
